LETTER 001
TO SOMEONE I USED TO KNOW.

I don't know if you would understand these words now. Maybe you wouldn't even recognize the person writing them.

There was a time when I thought some people were meant to stay. I was wrong.

Some people come into your life to teach you something, not to remain in it forever.

I don't regret knowing you. I just stopped waiting for you to become someone you were never meant to be.

I hope life is kind to you. Even if our paths never cross again.

— Muhammed.R
